IDEHA: Interoperability
Data Ecosystems for
Humanitarian Assistance

In 2025 and 2026, IOM, with the support of ECHO, conducted a pilot project, primarily focused on South Sudan, on understanding and improving the interoperability of registration and beneficiary management systems.

This mini website is to: illustrate the challenge the project addresses, and its objectives; showcase key project outputs; provide a knowledge management repository for the project activities; and act as a point of reference for people and organizations interested in the interoperability of registration and beneficiary management systems.

The problem and the approach

[ FIG. 02 ]
01 / What the project addressed

Disjointed registration & beneficiary management

Non-functioning, slow or impossible deduplication of lists;

Clear, timely referrals across organizations are rare or non-existent;

Disjointed vulnerability criteria and targeting;

Data sharing that can’t keep pace with operational realities;

Large variance in registration standards, practices and system capabilities.

02 / What IDEHA worked on

Standardize registration, reuse across organizations

IDEHA piloted a collaborative ecosystem for coordinated registration, targeting, and delivery, by using a standardized core registration form, with accompanying data protection impact assessment and data sharing agreements

03 / The vision

A federated ecosystem

No deduplication of registration activities.

Data moves from where it collected to where it's needed.

Intersectoral, inter-agency referral.

Harmonized vulnerability-based targeting.

04 / What the project did

Five Activites

  • FrameworkDevelopment of IOM Framework for Registration, Targeting, aid delivery, and data sharing for humanitarian response.
  • BRaVe system developmentEnhancement of the BRaVe system for agility and interoperability with various systems, and it is accessible to partners based on their digital maturity.
  • Mapping AnalysisMapping of all registration partners and activities.
  • Single Registration FormPrepare a draft Single Registration Form (SRF) to standardize the data points and methodology.
  • PilotImplementation of a pilot to exchange the data at least with one partner through interoperability to deduplicate the beneficiary list.
